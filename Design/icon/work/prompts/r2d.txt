CONCEPT — "Handoff to the dash": a wide car center display pane (about 2:1), luminous blue→cyan glass, the hero, seated centered on the crest of a smooth dark dashboard curve that sweeps across the lower quarter (satin graphite, thin cyan rim light). Overlapping its upper-left corner from BEHIND: a smaller MacBook-proportioned (16:10) frosted-glass pane, cooler and dimmer, clearly a second screen. A single luminous ribbon of light arcs out of the small pane and sweeps into the wide display, as if the laptop's picture is flowing onto it. Frontal, calm, centered, iconic, Apple-grade glass.
