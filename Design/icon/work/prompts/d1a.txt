CONCEPT — "Handoff panes": two overlapping frosted-glass display panes. Upper-left: a smaller MacBook-proportioned pane (16:10), slightly tilted back in space. Lower-right and in front: a WIDE landscape car-center-display pane (about 2:1), larger, glowing from within with the blue→cyan gradient light (no UI, no pictures, just luminous gradient "screen light"). A single smooth luminous signal arc / ribbon of light sweeps from the laptop pane into the wide pane, like the image is flowing across. Abstract and iconic. The wide pane is the hero.
