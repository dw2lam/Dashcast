CONCEPT — "Arranged displays" (like the macOS Displays-arrangement pictogram): two crisp rounded-rectangle screens side by side on the white glass, separated by a small even gap, their vertical CENTERS aligned: on the left a smaller 16:10 Mac screen, on the right a WIDE car display (about 2.3:1), the wide one clearly larger. Both filled with ONE continuous blue #0A6CFF → cyan #22C3F3 gradient that flows across the gap from left to right, so the picture visibly extends from the Mac onto the wide display. Thin white glass rims, soft shadows below each. The pair is centered as a group. Minimal, iconic.
